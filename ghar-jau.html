How it works

From last call to your driveway.

Four steps, built for the moment you're actually least equipped to plan ahead.

1

Request a driver

Open the app, drop your pin, and tell us where you're headed. Takes less time than waiting for the bill.

Under 60 seconds
2

A verified driver arrives — on foot or by bike

Your matched driver reaches your location, ID and rating visible in-app before they arrive.

Typically 10–20 minutes in Kathmandu Valley
3

They drive your car — with you in it

You hand over the keys, sit back, and your own vehicle takes you home. Live GPS tracking runs the whole way, shareable with a friend or family member.

Route + ETA visible throughout
4

Pay in-app, rate your driver

No cash changes hands. Rate the trip so the next passenger knows exactly who's picking them up.

Automatic, in seconds
Safety, by design

Trust is the entire product.

You're handing a stranger the keys to your car. Every part of Ghar Jau is built to make that feel obviously safe, not just claimed to be.

Verified drivers only

License checks, background verification, and in-person onboarding before anyone drives your car.

Ratings both ways

Drivers rate passengers, passengers rate drivers. A track record before you ever meet.

Live GPS tracking

Every trip is tracked start to finish. Share your route in real time with someone you trust.

Cashless payment

Pay by card or wallet in the app. No cash, no awkward fare negotiation late at night.
